Frequently asked questions

Does Z Media Production shoot weddings outside Kolkata?+

Yes — we cover weddings both within Kolkata and outstation across India. Travel, food and hotel expenses for the team are charged extra for outstation bookings.

What is included in the wedding packages?+

Every package includes full wedding video coverage, a wedding teaser, a printed album, and two reels for social media. The Classic and Premium tiers add cinematic colour grading, a second videographer and photographer, a makeup shoot, drone coverage and a pre-wedding shoot day.

How do I book Z Media Production?+

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 97485 02520, call the same number, email zmediaproductions1989@gmail.com, or use the enquiry form on the Services page — just pick your package and we'll take it from there.

Where is the studio located?+

We're based at Matiaburj High School, B-148/2E, Lane, Masjid Talab Basti, Garden Reach, Kolkata, West Bengal 700024. Full directions are on our Contact page.
